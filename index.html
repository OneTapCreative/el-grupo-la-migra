QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do you perform?

Weddings, quinceañeras, birthdays, private parties, family celebrations, festivals, community events, and custom bookings.

How much does it cost to book?

Pricing depends on the event date, location, performance length, setup needs, and event details. Use the quote form below for custom pricing.

How far do you travel?

We are based in Stockton, California and generally travel up to approximately 60 miles for events. Travel availability depends on the event date, venue address, route, and schedule. If your location is near the edge of the service area, send us the address and we will confirm availability.

How early should we book?

Popular weekends can fill quickly, so earlier is better. Send the date as soon as possible to check availability.

Can we request specific songs or music?

Song and performance requests can be discussed during booking. Final availability depends on the set list and event format.
